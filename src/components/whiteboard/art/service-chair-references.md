# Service chair front views

`ServiceChair.astro` follows the front-view photographs in the manufacturer's product sheets. The SVGs are hand-drawn elevations, not product configurator renders. All seats and frames are symmetric about the centre line. Colours are adapted to the site's palette; Bambino retains its natural wood appearance.

- **PantoSwing-LuPo:** [VS school chair sheet](https://web-media.vs.de/file/cms/doc/PSwing-LuPo-Schul_TY_DE.pdf), page 1, upper-right and lower-middle photographs. Circular grip, broad top, tapered waist, separate curved seat lip, two tubular front rails and floor crossbar.
- **JUMPER Air Four:** [VS school chair sheet](https://web-media.vs.de/file/cms/doc/Jumper-Air-S-Four_TY_DE.pdf), page 1, upper-right photograph. Broad polygonal back, low triangular grip opening, shaped seat sides, four-leg frame. No top grip or decorative perforation pattern.
- **PantoMove-LuPo:** [VS replacement parts sheet](https://web-media.vs.de/file/cms/doc/ZBE-45-076-08_EN.pdf), page 1, lower-left front photograph. LuPo shell, height adjustment mechanism and lever, five-star base and casters.
- **JUMPER Ply Four:** [VS school chair sheet](https://web-media.vs.de/file/cms/doc/Jumper-Ply-S-Four_TY_DE.pdf), page 1, upper-middle and lower-right photographs. Broad plywood back, high triangular grip, narrow connection to a separate seat, four-leg frame.
- **Bambino:** [VS chair sheet](https://web-media.vs.de/file/cms/doc/Bambino_TY_DE.pdf), page 1, lower-left photograph. Solid wooden frame, curved top rail, large open space below it, separate seat. No grip slot or lower leg stretcher.

The chairs share a 400 × 500 view box and a floor line around y = 488. Bambino has a lower backrest to convey its smaller size. The service descriptions remain separate from the decorative chair illustrations.
